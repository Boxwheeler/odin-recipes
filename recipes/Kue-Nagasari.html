Nagasari Cake

Nagasari cake is a traditional Indonesia's steamed cake made with soft rice flour and sweet banana with coconut milk, here is the step:

Ingredients

  • 40g (2.5 tablespoons) tapioca starch
  • 200g (1.5 cups) white rice flour
  • 150g (3/4 cup) granulated sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 800ml (3.5 cups) thin coconut milk
  • 3-4 bananas (preferably pisang raja or saba)
  • Banana leaves (cut into rectangles, roughly \(\sim 15 \times 20\text{ cm}\))
  • 1-2 pandan leaves (optional, knotted)

Steps

  1. In a large pot or saucepan, combine the rice flour, tapioca starch, sugar, salt, and thin coconut milk. Whisk until smooth and strain to remove any lumps. Add the knotted pandan leaf.
  2. Cook the mixture over medium-low heat, stirring constantly. The batter will thicken into a smooth, shiny, and paste-like consistency (thick porridge texture). Remove from heat as soon as it thickens and let it cool slightly.
  3. Peel your bananas and slice them diagonally or into halves, depending on their size.
  4. Lay a banana leaf flat. Spoon about 2 tablespoons of the warm batter onto the center of the leaf, place a banana slice on top, and cover with another spoonful of batter.
  5. Fold the sides of the leaf inward, then fold the top and bottom ends toward the middle to create a neat, sealed rectangular packet.
  6. Place the packets in a steamer. Cover and steam over medium heat for about 20–25 minutes until fully cooked.
  7. Allow the cakes to cool down to room temperature so they set perfectly.
