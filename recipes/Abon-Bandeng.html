Abon Bandeng Fish Recipes

For This recipe we will make Fish Abon which is type of dry food that made of any type of meat and fish. For this recipe we will use Bandeng Fish, Here is the step:

  • 2 Bandeng Fish Presto (Fish you use high pressure cooker until the bone is soft and can be eaten)
  • 100 grams of Coconut Cream
  • 150 ml of Oil (any type of oil)
  • 6 cloves of red onion
  • 4 cloves of garlic
  • 1 big chilli (or more if you like spicy)
  • 1 piece of turmeric
  • Salt
  • MSG or any Umami
  • 1 teaspoon of sugar
  1. Wash the fish and then dissolve them until the meat so small.
  2. blend garlics, onions, chillies, turmeric together to make paste.
  3. heat the pan, then oil, after that put the paste until the smell coming out, then put coconut cream, and then saute until it's good enough, put salt, umami and sugar
  4. then put the fish to the pan and mix it until mixed together
  5. keep mixing until it's dry, after it's dry take it out and serve on the plate.
