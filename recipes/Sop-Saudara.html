Sop Sodara Recipes

One of Indonesian's traditional food, from one of villages, we will make this step by step:

Ingredients

  • 250 g beef shank or brisket (cut into cubes)
  • 200 g beef offal (tripe and lungs, cleaned and boiled)
  • 750 ml water
  • 1 stalk lemongrass (bruised)
  • 2 cm galangal (bruised)
  • 2 cm ginger (bruised)
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1.5 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 9 shallots
  • 5 cloves garlic
  • 3 candlenuts (roasted)
  • 1.5 tbsp coriander seeds (roasted)
  • 2 cm turmeric
  • 1/4 tsp cumin

Steps

  1. In a pot, boil the beef and offal in 750 ml of water until tender. Once tender, dice the offal and set aside. Keep the beef broth in the pot.
  2. Heat cooking oil in a pan. Sauté the ground spice paste, lemongrass, galangal, and ginger until fragrant.
  3. Pour the sautéed spices into the beef broth. Add the cinnamon, ground nutmeg, and salt. Cook on low heat until the flavors are perfectly infused.
  4. Arrange the rice vermicelli, diced offal, and beef in a serving bowl. Pour the hot broth over them.
  5. Garnish with green onions, fried shallots, potato patties, and crispy fried beef lungs. Serve hot with a side of ketupat or warm rice.
